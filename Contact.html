How long does buyer verification take?

Standard KYC and fund verification typically completes within 24 hours. VIP clients receive expedited 2-hour processing.

What happens if my bid wins?

A 10% escrow hold is immediately placed. You then have 48 hours to complete the legal paperwork and initiate the remaining wire transfer.

Are all physical assets authenticated?

Yes. Every physical item (sneakers, watches) undergoes a rigorous 3-step physical authentication process by our internal experts before being listed.
